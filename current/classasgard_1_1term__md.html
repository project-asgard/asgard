Detailed Description

template<typename P = default_precision>
class asgard::term_md< P >

Multidimensional term of the partial differential equation.

The term can be one of three modes:

  • a separable term consisting of a number of one-dimensional chains, one per dimension
  • an interpolation term, possibly non-linear and non-separable
  • a chain of separable or interpolation terms

Cannot create a separable term with all pterm_chain as identity. A chain can be build only of separable and interpolation terms, recursive chains are not allowed.
