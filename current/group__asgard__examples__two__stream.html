Detailed Description

Two stream instability
Solves the Vlasov-Poisson equation in a common example often called the two stream instability problem

where the electric field term depends on the Poisson equation

The equation represents the evolution of a charged particle field under the effects of self-induced electric field. The right-hand integral represents the density of the particles and creates non-linear coupling between the fields.
The focus of this example is the coupling with the electric field and Poisson solver.
This is still work-in-progress, the documentation needs more work.
