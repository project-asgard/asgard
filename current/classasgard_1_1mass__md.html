Detailed Description

template<typename P = default_precision>
class asgard::mass_md< P >

Separable mass term, i.e., num-dims volume 1d terms.

A regular term_1d that is volume or a term_md with with mass terms can depend on time, e.g., via moments, or can be negative in some areas of the domain. The mass-md term contains only volume terms that are time-independent and have an always positive coefficient.
